# Hostel Rules and Regulations

## 1. Entry and Exit
- Students must follow the hostel entry and exit timings.
- Late entry requires permission from the hostel warden.
- Students must sign the register when required.

## 2. Visitors
- Visitors must register at the hostel reception.
- Visitors are allowed only during permitted visiting hours.
- Visitors are not allowed inside student rooms without permission.

## 3. Cleanliness
- Students must keep their rooms and common areas clean.
- Waste must be disposed of in designated dustbins.
- Students must not damage hostel property.

## 4. Safety and Security
- Students must carry their hostel identity card.
- Electrical appliances must be used safely.
- Emergency situations must be reported to the hostel warden immediately.

## 5. Discipline
- Loud music and disturbances are not allowed during quiet hours.
- Students must respect other residents and hostel staff.
- Any violation of hostel rules may be reported to the hostel administration.

**Note:** This is mock hostel information created only for the MCP server assignment. It is not an official college hostel policy.
